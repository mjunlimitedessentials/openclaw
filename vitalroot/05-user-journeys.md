## Journey 2: "I'm Struggling Today" Flow (James)

**User:** James, 33, post-stroke recovery, dealing with depression and fatigue.

```
TRIGGER
│
└── It's Thursday afternoon. James hasn't opened the app in 5 days.
    Push notification: "No pressure, James. Just here if you need us."
    He opens the app.

HOME SCREEN
│
└── Sees: "I'm struggling today" button (always visible, prominent)
    He taps it.

STRUGGLING FLOW — SCREEN 1
│
├── Heading: "We're glad you're here."
├── Body: "Some days are just hard. That's real and it's okay.
│          Can you tell us a little about what's going on?"
└── Quick options:
    ○ I'm exhausted and can't do much today
    ○ I'm feeling hopeless or really down
    ○ I had a health setback
    ○ I just fell off track and feel bad about it
    ○ Something else

    → James selects: "I'm feeling hopeless or really down"

STRUGGLING FLOW — SCREEN 2 (Mood Assessment + Safety Check)
│
├── "Thank you for sharing that. We want to make sure you're okay."
├── "On a scale of 1–5, how are you feeling right now?"
│   1 = Very bad / 5 = Could be worse
│   → James selects: 2
│
└── Safety screening question (non-clinical language):
    "Sometimes when we're feeling really low, thoughts can get dark.
     Are you having any thoughts of hurting yourself?"
    ○ No, just feeling really down
    ○ Yes, and I'd like some support
    ○ I'm not sure / I don't want to answer

    → James selects: "No, just feeling really down"

STRUGGLING FLOW — SCREEN 3 (Empathetic Response)
│
├── Companion enters Empathy + Sadness-Aware mode:
│   "James, what you're carrying is a lot. Recovering from a stroke
│    while managing depression is genuinely hard — not something you
│    should be 'over' by now. You're not behind. You're still here,
│    and that matters."
│
├── Three micro-options:
│   ○ "Give me one tiny thing I can do right now"
│   ○ "Just talk to me for a few minutes"
│   ○ "Show me something helpful"
│
└── James selects: "Give me one tiny thing I can do right now"

MICRO-STEP SUGGESTION
│
├── "Here's one thing: drink a glass of water right now.
│    Not because it changes everything — just because your body
│    appreciates it, and small acts of care add up."
│
├── Then: "When you're ready — and only when you're ready —
│          here's a 5-minute seated stretch that might help your
│          body feel a little less stuck."
│          [Link: Seated shoulder and neck stretch, Level 0]
│
└── "You don't have to do anything else today. Showing up here counts."

FOLLOW-UP (Next Morning)
│
└── Push notification: "Good morning, James. Yesterday you showed up
    when it was hard. That's real strength. No pressure today —
    just here if you need us."
```
