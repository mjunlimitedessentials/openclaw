# 12 — Content Cluster Strategy

## Content Architecture Model

VitalRoot uses a **Pillar + Cluster + Supporting** model with four content tiers:

```
TIER 1: Pillar Pages (condition hubs, 2,500–4,000 words)
    │
    ├── TIER 2: Cluster Pages (condition sub-topics, 1,200–2,000 words)
    │       │
    │       ├── TIER 3: Supporting Articles (specific questions, 800–1,500 words)
    │       │
    │       └── TIER 4: Programmatic Pages (recipe × condition, FAQ × condition)
    │
    └── Cross-cluster: Recipe pages, Exercise pages, Herb pages, Local resource pages
```

## Content Differentiation Matrix

For each major content type, we lead with what competitors miss:

| Content Type | Generic Competitor | VitalRoot Differentiator |
|-------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Diabetes recipe | Grilled chicken and steamed broccoli | Soul food, Caribbean, Latin adaptations |
| Kidney diet guide | Generic food avoid list | Cultural food substitutions, caregiver guide |
| Herb page | Promotional benefits focus | Evidence level, drug interactions, kidney risk flag |
| Heart health exercise | Cardio 5x/week instruction | Seated options, condition-specific modifications |
| Motivation content | "Just stay positive!" | Setback acknowledgment, cultural context, real empathy |
| Local resources | Google Maps link | Insurance, language, sliding scale, condition specialty |
