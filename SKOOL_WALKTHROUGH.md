## STEP 0 — DECIDE THE COMMUNITY POSITIONING

> Don't build until you've decided what it *is*. Three options — pick one.

**Option A — Free community + Premium tier** (recommended for Y1)
- Free: anyone can join. Builds top-of-funnel and trust.
- Premium: $97/month. Includes weekly office hours with Mona, exclusive templates, certification track.
- *Trade-off:* slower revenue, faster brand growth.

**Option B — Paid-only community** ($47–$97/mo flat)
- One tier. Higher commitment from members.
- *Trade-off:* smaller community, deeper engagement.

**Option C — Community bundled with Anchored Pro / Enterprise** (free for clients)
- Included as part of the $497/mo or $1,497/mo retainer.
- *Trade-off:* no standalone revenue, but huge retention lift.

> **Recommendation:** Start with **Option A**. Capture top-of-funnel free, convert to premium, eventually bundle.

## STEP 8 — iOS DAILY OPS (SKOOL APP)

The Skool app is genuinely good on iPhone. Here's the routine:

- **Morning (5 min):** open app, reply to overnight comments, like new posts
- **Midday (5 min):** post one tactic or question
- **Evening (5 min):** check engagement scores, plan tomorrow's post
- **Weekly:** record the office hours from your iPhone or Mac, upload to Classroom

> Push notifications: ON for new members + comments. OFF for likes (or you'll get overwhelmed).

## COMMON GOTCHAS

| Problem | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Low engagement | Post questions, not statements. Ask members to share their score. |
| Members leave fast | Welcome them by name within 24 hours of joining. Personally. |
| Community feels empty | Move free members to a separate space — the main feed should always feel active |
| Premium upgrade rate low | Run a $47 founding offer with a deadline |
| Skool app crashes | Update iOS + app — known issues clear up fast |
