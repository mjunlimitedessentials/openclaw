Frequently asked

Will the AI sound robotic to my members or clients?

No. We use modern voice models and tune them to your culture. We also identify the agent as AI upfront — that's required by law in several states and it actually builds more trust than pretending.

Is this TCPA-compliant?

Yes. Every text and call has consent captured, timestamped, and stored. Opt-out is automatic. We've built compliance into the system, not bolted it on.

What if my team isn't tech-savvy?

The MJUEM 4-phase methodology assumes that. We train your admin in plain English over 1 session, give you a one-page cheat sheet, and stay on retainer if you need us.
