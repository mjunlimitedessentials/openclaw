## 5-EMAIL NURTURE — DAY 0 → DAY 10

> **Format note:** All emails come from Mona personally. Plain-text style, not designed templates. From: `mona@anchoredaiops.com`.

### Email 1 — Day 0 (immediate, automatic on opt-in)
**Subject:** Your AI Readiness Check is inside ⚓
**Open:**
> Hi {{first_name}} — your check is attached and ready.
**Body:**
- Link to the PDF + button
- One-line: *"Score yourself in 3 minutes. Reply to this email with your score if you want me to weigh in personally."*
- Soft CTA: *"Or skip ahead and book a 15-min Anchored Check-In: [link]"*
- Signature with photo

### Email 2 — Day 1
**Subject:** Why I built this (and what it cost the last pastor I worked with)
**Body:** Mona's origin story (60 sec read). Why faith-led & community-rooted businesses get sold AI that doesn't fit. What it cost one church to ignore the voicemail problem for 6 months. End with: *"Did your Readiness Check surface a 'voice' or 'data' problem? Hit reply — I read every one."*

### Email 3 — Day 3
**Subject:** The voicemail problem (and the 30-day fix)
**Body:** Direct pain agitation for Avatar A. Concrete numbers: *"240-member church + 30–50 voicemails a week + 1 part-time admin = the math doesn't work."* Introduce Agent 508 in plain English (NOT product specs — outcome language). CTA: book the call.

### Email 4 — Day 5
**Subject:** A case study you'll see yourself in
**Body:** Hypothetical (until we have real ones) — "Pastor L." composite case: voicemail problem → Anchored Starter → 30-day result. Permission to use as a teaching story. CTA: book the call.

### Email 5 — Day 7
**Subject:** Last call (no pressure, but a quick ask)
**Body:** Direct: *"You took the readiness check 7 days ago. If you want a free 15-min walkthrough of your score — book here. If not, no worries — I'll send you something useful next month."* Two buttons: book / not now.

### Email 6 (optional) — Day 10
**Subject:** ⚓ Closing your file (for now)
**Body:** "I won't keep emailing you weekly. I'll move you to my monthly note where I share one tactic from the field. If you ever want to revisit, just reply."

## POST-CALL BRANCHES

### Branch 1 — WON (signs same call or within 48 hours)
- Stripe checkout link (GHL-hosted)
- Onboarding email triggers (Welcome PWA setup)
- Calendar invite for kickoff call
- Slack channel created (Pro / Enterprise only)

### Branch 2 — NURTURE (interested, not ready)
- Tag: `nurture-30day`
- 30-day cadence: 4 emails over 30 days (case studies, mini-tactics)
- After 30 days: ask for a second call

### Branch 3 — LOST (no fit / not interested)
- Tag: `lost-q1-2026`
- Quarterly re-engage email
- Ask: *"Anything change? Or know someone this fits?"* (referral capture)
